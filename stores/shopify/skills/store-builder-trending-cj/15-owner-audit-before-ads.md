# Level 15 — Owner audit before paid ads (v2.6)

Run this after Level 14 passes and **before any money goes into ads**,
and again after every major change. It's the store owner's view: is
this store ready to take paid traffic and turn it into profit? It's
adapted from an outside audit format Itzik brought in, and it sits on
top of the rules in Levels 01–14 — it never overrides them (pricing is
still the Level 03 rule; images still Levels 01 rule 8–9).

## Ground rules

- **Don't hunt for problems.** If something works, say it works and
  mark it 🟢. A short honest report beats a long padded one.
- **No invented data.** No made-up conversion rates, traffic or
  competitor numbers. Every number is either measured (with where and
  when) or labelled **ESTIMATE**.
- **Every finding names the fix** and the level that owns it.

## The three marks

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🔴 **Must fix before ads** | Loses sales or money, or hurts trust. Ads on top of it waste the budget. |
| 🟡 **Recommended** | Would improve conversion, AOV or trust, but doesn't block ads. |
| 🟢 **Fine** | Works. Leave it alone. |

## What to check

1. **Homepage — the 5–10 second test.** On a 375px phone, above the
   fold: what does the store sell, who is it for, why buy here? Brand
   line: "Everything baby needs. All in one place." — does the page
   deliver on it with 3 products, or does it need a narrower line?
2. **Per product** (one block each), mark each row:

   | Area | What to check |
   |---|---|
   | Pricing | Current price vs Level 03 record: landed cost, market median, margin $ and %, Buy 2 margin. Is the $ profit per order enough to pay for a sale from ads? |
   | Product page | Headline, first screen, benefit clarity, answers the obvious questions (age, size, safety, materials, what's in the box). |
   | Images | CJ-only, clean, hero shows use (rule 8); **picture follows every variant choice** (rule 9). |
   | Description | Honest, scannable, no supplier text. |
   | Offer & bundle | Buy 2 makes sense for this product and is priced right. |
   | Trust | Reviews with photos (≥15), shipping and return clarity, real contact. |
   | Paid-ads potential | Is there a visual "hook" for a video ad? Price point impulse-friendly? |

3. **Ad path.** Ad → landing page → product → cart → checkout, on a
   phone. Every step loads fast, keeps the price and choice the
   customer saw, and the cart total matches the tier. Don't complete a
   payment.
4. **AOV.** Buy 2, cross-sell, free-shipping threshold — real and
   honest.
5. **Mobile first.** Tap targets, sticky add-to-cart, no horizontal
   scroll, images load fast.
6. **SEO basics.** Title and meta description per page, alt text,
   sitemap without hidden products.
7. **Trust pages.** Shipping, returns, contact, privacy — present and
   true to how CJ actually ships.

## Report format

1. A table: every area × every product, with its mark.
2. Counts: how many 🔴 / 🟡 / 🟢.
3. **All 🔴 in priority order**, each with: what's wrong, why it
   costs money, the fix, the owning level.
4. **Top 3 changes** that move the most money.
5. One line: **ready for ads — yes / no.** Yes only with zero 🔴.

Save the report to `store-profiles/alphaforbaby/audits/<date>.md`.
Any 🔴 that is a new kind of bug goes into Level 10 with a Level 14
test the same day.
